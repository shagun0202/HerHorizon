UNDERSTANDING PCOS

Know Your Body. Understand PCOS.

Understanding PCOS can help women recognise symptoms and seek appropriate medical guidance.

Irregular Periods

Changes in menstrual cycle patterns and irregular periods can be associated with PCOS.

Skin & Hair Changes

Acne, excessive facial hair growth and hair thinning may occur in some women.

Weight Changes

Some women with PCOS experience weight changes and metabolic difficulties.

These symptoms can have multiple causes. Symptoms alone cannot confirm PCOS.
